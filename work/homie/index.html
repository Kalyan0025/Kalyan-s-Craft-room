Homie.AI · A screen-aware AI coach

A coach that helps in the moment, and knows when to stay quiet.

Homie sits on top of the software you are learning, points at what to do, then steps back on purpose until you can do it alone. The hard part was not the help. It was the rules for when to give it.

02 · Problem

Help lives outside the work, so people leave to find it.

Every learner we watched left the tool at least once. The answer was always somewhere else: a video, a doc, a forum, a chat.

03 · Research

Capable people, blocked by fragmented support.

Eight interviews across Figma, Unity, After Effects and other complex tools, plus a literature review, questionnaires and observation.

Who we designed for

MayaThe overwhelmed learner

Early-career designer in Figma or After Effects. Needs calm, step-by-step support that builds confidence.

AlexThe efficiency driver

Self-taught developer who hates friction. Needs precise, minimal guidance.

SofiaThe creative transitioner

Moving from beginner tools to professional software. Needs light guidance that protects her flow.

04 · Ideation

One orb, one task at a time.

Five assistant models explored: overlay, sidebar, cursor-following, voice and visual cues. The floating orb won because it is visible without becoming another distraction.

05 · Guardrails

A coach that knows when to stay quiet.

An AI that watches your screen can help too much, too early, or in the wrong place. Our information-architecture lead wrote the rules that stop it. Five roles, one floor, a fade-out, an honest ladder and a way to ask when unsure.

1 · Five roles, one floor

Only one speaks at a time. Tap a role.

If two want the floor: Repair › Guide › Explainer

2 · Who speaks right now

The orb asks five questions in order. The first yes wins. Pick a moment.

01Paused, or in a restricted app?Stay silent
02Error on screen, or the user looks lost?Repair speaks
03Did the user ask something?Explainer answers
04Same step three times, or three hints?Support steps down a stage
05Next step needed, or idle over 15 seconds?Guide speaks

3 · Support fades on purpose

Across about ten operations, help steps back. Drag through a module.

User can shift one stage either wayRepair forces S1 while fixingThree hints on one step: back one stage
