Active Zen · Wellness app · Brand and interaction design

Meditation for people who cannot sit still.

Active Zen syncs breath with movement, so a run, a swim or a clean-up becomes the practice. This page is about how the brand got there and how the system moves.

interaction design

01 · The idea

Stillness is hard. Movement is easy.

Problem

Sitting meditation fails the people who struggle with stillness, and nothing in the category fits a busy, moving day.

Solution

Active meditation: breathe in sync with a run, a swim or a clean-up, guided by one calm, colour-coded app.

02 · Brand

Start from the lotus, end with a runner inside it.

Meditation imagery already speaks in chakras and mandalas. Active Zen keeps that language and adds the one thing it lacked: a body in motion.

01Borrow the symbol

Chakras and celestial patterns stand for balance, energy and mindfulness.

02Build one shape

A petal ring and two inner rings, drawn once and reused everywhere.

03Put a body inside

A flat, faceless figure per activity, so anyone can see themselves in it.

04Give each a colour

One hue per activity on a dark base, so the app is sorted by colour first.

03 · Mandala system

One mark, drawn ten ways.

  • 01
    Petals

    The lotus outline. It stays constant, so every activity reads as one family.

  • 02
    Rings

    Two concentric rings frame the figure and become the breathing timer later.

  • 03
    Figure

    The only part that changes: runner, swimmer, cyclist, lifter, dancer.

04 · Colour

The hue follows you from tile to timer.

Each activity owns a colour. It tints the icon, the info screen and the weekly chart, so a glance tells you what you did and when.

05 · Components

Small parts, built to repeat.

Every activity is assembled from the same few pieces, straight from the design file.

Icon ring

Two sizes per activity: swim, cycle, clean, art.

Breath ring

The petals fill as the session moves on. Four states, one colour per activity.

Ring in motion

Eight frames turn the ring while you breathe.

Instruction cards

A doodle and one line per step, from stand tall to relax.

06 · Micro-interactions

The timer is the breath.

  • Breath ringThe mandala as a timer

    The same rings from the logo run the session, with an inhale-and-exhale cue inside.

  • HapticsVibration on breath cues

    Eyes stay on the road. The phone taps the rhythm instead.

  • Voice and soundOptional guidance

    Calming spoken steps, plus a music pick.

  • WearableConnect a device

    Heart rate can join the session.
